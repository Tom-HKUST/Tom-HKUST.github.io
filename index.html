About Xujiang Xiang

I am Xujiang Xiang, a PhD student in the Department of Electronic and Computer Engineering (ECE) at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST). My research focuses on computer architecture and efficient hardware systems for emerging AI workloads, including hardware acceleration for visual generative intelligence, GPGPU architecture, and SRAM-based computing-in-memory (SRAM-CIM).

I have published work at major venues in computer engineering and solid-state circuits, including HPCA, MICRO, and ISSCC. My selected publications and additional research experience are available in my CV.
